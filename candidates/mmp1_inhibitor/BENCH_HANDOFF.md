## Caveats & recommended assays
- **Stereo-blind model**: the ECFP fingerprints ignore stereochemistry, so the QSAR cannot rank stereoisomers — the picks above collapse to **2 distinct constitutional scaffolds** (CAND-04389-type, MW 383; CAND-04423-type, MW 409), each shown as 2–3 stereoisomers with identical predictions. Stereochemistry must be resolved experimentally (or by docking).
- **Multiple chelators / heavy alert load**: these carry a hydroxamic acid AND an N-hydroxypyridinone (both metal-binders) — strong predicted binding, but a real selectivity/tox/PK liability. This is the classic MMP-inhibitor failure chemotype; weight accordingly.
- **Predicted potency/selectivity** (QSAR); selectivity = difference of two model predictions → errors compound. Triage prior, not truth.
- **Synthesizability not assessed** (BRICS fragments). Most carry structural alerts (the reactive zinc-binding group + recombined fragments) — chemist review required before synthesis.
- **Applicability**: in-domain but at the looser edge (Tanimoto ~0.5–0.6 to nearest known) → more extrapolation than close analogs.
- **Confirm with**: (1) MMP-1 fluorogenic inhibition assay (IC50); (2) MMP-2/3/9/13 panel for the predicted selectivity; (3) standard counter-screens / cytotoxicity given the chelator chemotype.
